Help centre

Questions? We've got you.

Quick answers to common onboarding questions. Search a question or open a topic below.

How long will onboarding take?
Timing depends on how quickly requested information is provided, partner review, any additional checks, and whether more documents are needed. We’ll show the current stage here. If a review is taking longer than expected, the tracker should say that Volopay or its partner is working on it rather than asking you to repeat a completed step.
What do you need from me right now?
Check My actions for the exact outstanding item and its instructions. If the status says Waiting on Volopay / partner, there is no action for you unless the tracker explicitly requests one.
Who can see my documents?
Documents should be available only to authorised people who need them for onboarding, verification, or compliance review. The exact access controls, processors, and retention rules should be confirmed in Volopay’s official privacy and onboarding policies.
Why does each employee need to complete KYC?
KYC means Know Your Customer. Identity checks help verify that the person using the platform is who they claim to be and support applicable compliance requirements. The exact people who must complete KYC can depend on the account setup, role, and applicable rules.
When do you need video KYC, and why?
Video KYC may be requested when an applicable verification or compliance process requires an additional identity check. It is not safe to assume every user will need it: follow the request shown in your onboarding instructions. If it is required, the tracker should explain who needs to complete it and how to proceed.
What is MDC?
In this onboarding flow, MDC is the stage labelled for basic documents and CSM assignment. Use the specific checklist provided by your onboarding representative to confirm which documents are required for your account.
What is partner review?
At this stage, the partner checks the business. The review may take time. If no further information is needed from you, the tracker should show that the next action is with Volopay or its partner.
What is ADC?
In the supplied workflow, ADC is a stage that occurs only if the partner asks for more information. If it is triggered, the tracker should list the specific additional item required and show that it is waiting on you.
What happens after platform access is created?
The supplied workflow shows client creation and platform access before User KYC. After User KYC, the client is marked live. Follow the status and instructions shown for your account.
What if the review is delayed or there's a technical issue?
You should see a clear update explaining that the next action is with Volopay, what is currently happening at a high level, and whether you need to do anything. If a technical issue affects progress, the tracker should show that the team is resolving it and provide a next-update expectation when one is available. It should not expose internal-only notes or promise an unverified resolution time.
